01 · Thesis — Beyond the Number

From knowing the air is hazardous to tracing why.

The Mathematics of Masking — CPCB NAQI Aggregation CPCB 2014 [2]
AQI = max( IPM2.5, IPM10, INO2, ISO2, ICO, IO3 )

A station reports only the maximum sub-index of a single dominant pollutant — the index communicates severity for public awareness, not composition, geography, or origin. WHAT THE SCALAR AQI CAN HIDE

A maximum sub-index can obscure the simultaneous presence of other pollutants. Particulate matter acts as an inhalation carrier for adsorbed nitrogen and sulfur species — a compound dynamic a single number cannot express.

Statutory Boundary DECISION SUPPORT ≠ AUTHORITY

The analytical pipeline prepares structured recommendations — draft inspection checklists, prioritized candidate information, notice templates — for authorized officers. Physical inspection, formal statutory orders, and enforcement remain the exclusive domain of authorized municipal and State Board officials under the Air (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act, 1981 [11].
